# Ecosystem Impact

The MUSE Foundation of Rhode Island strengthens cultural infrastructure and community ecosystems by:

- Elevating Black history and food culture through events like the Eve of Juneteenth and Taste of Juneteenth New England.
- Providing platforms for artistic expression such as the MSVL Bridge Sunset Concert Series.
- Fostering economic empowerment via partnerships with local institutions and sponsors.

These efforts contribute to a resilient and collaborative ecosystem that supports artists, entrepreneurs, and communities across Rhode Island.
