# Press Bio

Juan K. Wilson Jr. is an ecosystem architect, philanthropist, and cultural strategist whose work bridges community impact and creative brand innovation. He leads The MUSE Foundation of Rhode Island and maintains creative partnerships with araabMUZIK and InMusic/Akai Professional.

Juan serves in leadership roles across major institutions including the Providence Public Library, United Way of Rhode Island, the Rhode Island Foundation, and the Swearer Center at Brown University.

He is the recipient of the 2025 Christiana Carteaux Bannister Award, the 2025 Pawtucket Black History Month Impact Award, and the 2024 Eastside Wave Maker Award.
