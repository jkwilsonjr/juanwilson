# About Juan K. Wilson Jr.

Juan K. Wilson Jr. is an ecosystem architect, philanthropist, cultural strategist, and creative brand partner whose work bridges community impact and commercial innovation. He is the Founder & CEO of The MUSE Foundation of Rhode Island and the architect of the state’s multi‑day Juneteenth ecosystem.

Juan’s work spans nonprofit leadership, civic engagement, creative direction, and brand partnerships with global artists and companies including araabMUZIK and InMusic/Akai Professional.

He builds systems that uplift communities, strengthen institutions, and expand opportunity across Rhode Island.
