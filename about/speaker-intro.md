# Speaker Introduction

Juan K. Wilson Jr. is an ecosystem architect, philanthropist, and cultural strategist whose work is reshaping Rhode Island’s cultural and community landscape. He is the Founder & CEO of The MUSE Foundation of Rhode Island and the architect of the state’s multi-day Juneteenth ecosystem.

Juan also maintains creative partnerships with global producer araabMUZIK and InMusic/Akai Professional. His leadership extends across major institutions including the Providence Public Library, United Way of Rhode Island, and the Rhode Island Foundation.

He is a recipient of the Bannister Award, the Pawtucket Impact Award, and the Eastside Wave Maker Award.
