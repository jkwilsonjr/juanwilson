# Grant-Ready Organizational Narrative

The MUSE Foundation of Rhode Island builds cultural infrastructure through programming, partnerships, and community-centered initiatives that elevate Black history, food culture, artistic expression, and economic empowerment.

Flagship programs include:
- The Eve of Juneteenth (EOJ)
- Taste of Juneteenth New England
- MSVL Bridge Sunset Concert Series

Strategic partners:
- Hope & Main
- Providence Arts, Culture & Tourism
- Providence Parks Department
- Brown Broadcasting Service (WBRU)

Sponsors:
- Providence Tourism Council
- Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Rhode Island

MUSE’s work is strengthened by Juan’s leadership roles across major institutions including the Providence Public Library, United Way of Rhode Island, the Rhode Island Foundation, and the Swearer Center at Brown University.

MUSE has been recognized through awards such as the Bannister Award, Eastside Wave Maker Award, and Philanthropy Partner of the Year.
